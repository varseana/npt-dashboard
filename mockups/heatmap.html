Weekly NPT

One square per week, the whole year. Months run left to right; the weeks of each month stack downward (GitHub-style). Click a week to jump to it.

Hover a week to preview - click to select.

Horizontal matrix like GitHub: each column is a month (left to right), each row a week within it stacking down; empty padding squares close the rectangle. Levels are a continuous intensity ramp (not 3 flat colors): faint green as the week fills toward its threshold, red once over, near-invisible with little/no NPT. Month names kept very faint. Only real weeks with data are clickable; no-data weeks are shown but not clickable.
